# Directory & Citation Quality Audit for YoHomeFix

**Prepared:** 2026  
**Subject:** US directory/citation site suitability for a national home services lead generation website  
**Status:** Draft — awaiting approval before any submission

---

## Executive Summary

YoHomeFix is a **national lead generation website** that routes homeowner calls to contractors via a tracked phone number (`(844) 934-4386`). It is **not** a local plumbing company with a physical office, staff, or service vehicles. This distinction is critical because most traditional local SEO citation infrastructure — Google Business Profile, Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing Places — is designed for businesses that make **in-person contact with customers** and explicitly **excludes lead generation companies**.

The audit below categorizes directories by their real value to YoHomeFix's model, not by generic "best citation" lists that assume a local brick-and-mortar business.

**Bottom line:** Manual submission is worthwhile for **6–8 directories** that provide entity corroboration, trust signals, or AI-search visibility. The expected SEO benefit is **modest but real** — primarily through brand entity signals, AI citation likelihood, and link-profile diversity rather than direct PageRank or local pack rankings. Avoid all automated submission services, link farms, and low-quality SEO directories.

## Final Recommendation: Is Manual Submission Worthwhile?

### Yes — for a targeted set of 4–6 directories.

**Submit to these (in priority order):**

1. **Crunchbase** — Free, dofollow DR 91 link, entity verification, AI visibility. ~30 min.
2. **Trustpilot** — Free, DR 93, trust signal, review rich snippets. ~20 min.
3. **LinkedIn Company Page** — Free, DR 98, entity corroboration. ~20 min.
4. **Facebook Business Page** — Free, DR 96, social proof. ~15 min.
5. **BBB** (if budget allows $300–$1,000/yr) — AI trust signal, brand credibility. ~1 hour + ongoing.
6. **Foursquare** (if you have any business address) — Data distribution. ~15 min.

**Total time investment:** ~2 hours for the free directories.

### Expected SEO Benefit

| Benefit Type | Estimate |
|---|---|
| **Direct PageRank** | Minimal. Only Crunchbase provides a dofollow link. The rest are nofollow. |
| **Entity/Brand Signals** | **Moderate.** 4–6 high-DR profiles create entity corroboration that helps Google's Knowledge Graph and AI systems recognize YoHomeFix as a legitimate business entity. |
| **AI Search Visibility** | **Moderate.** Trustpilot + BBB + Crunchbase are referenced by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini when evaluating business trustworthiness. 2026 data shows nofollow brand mentions have 3× higher correlation with AI Overview visibility than dofollow backlinks. |
| **Link Profile Diversity** | **Moderate.** A natural link profile includes 25–35% nofollow links. These directories contribute to a healthy ratio. |
| **Local Pack Rankings** | **Zero.** YoHomeFix is ineligible for Google Business Profile, so local pack rankings are not achievable through directory citations. |
| **Branded Search SERP** | **Moderate.** Crunchbase, LinkedIn, Trustpilot, and Facebook profiles will occupy SERP real estate for "YoHomeFix" branded searches, pushing down irrelevant results. |
| **Referral Traffic** | **Low.** Expect minimal direct traffic from these profiles. The value is in signals, not clicks. |

### What NOT to do

- **Do not** create a Google Business Profile — lead-gen companies are explicitly ineligible
- **Do not** list on Yelp — lead-gen businesses are explicitly excluded
- **Do not** use automated/bulk submission services — they submit to link farms and create NAP inconsistencies
- **Do not** pursue low-quality directories (Hotfrog, MerchantCircle, Brownbook, etc.) — zero to negative value
- **Do not** list on Angi or Thumbtack — these are competitor lead marketplaces, not citation directories
- **Do not** attempt to create a Wikipedia article unless YoHomeFix genuinely meets notability guidelines
- **Do not** pay for YP.com, Houzz Pro, or any directory's premium listing for SEO purposes
