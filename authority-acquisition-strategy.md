# YoHomeFix Authority Acquisition Strategy

**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Scope:** Commercial keyword competitor authority analysis — no link building, no outreach, no purchases.  
**Purpose:** Identify where top-ranking plumbing competitors earn authority and prioritize citation / backlink opportunities for YoHomeFix.

---

## Executive Summary

Authority is the main remaining ranking barrier for YoHomeFix. The on-page H1/title changes are live and technically sound, but national and local competitors outrank primarily because of stronger off-site signals: **BBB accreditation**, **industry association membership**, **high-authority home-service directory listings**, **local citations**, and **real local review profiles**. This report ranks every opportunity by expected SEO impact and effort.

## 10. Link Gap Analysis vs. YoHomeFix

YoHomeFix currently has:
- **Strong on-page SEO** (titles, H1s, content, schema, sitemap, internal linking).
- **Domain age / authority unknown**, but likely low because it lacks the following:
- **No BBB profile visible** — major trust gap.
- **No real local address or license numbers** — blocks many citation sources and local pack eligibility.
- **No Google Business Profile / Yelp / Angi / Thumbtack profiles** — missing the core citation foundation.
- **No PHCC or state association membership** — missing industry trust signals.
- **No PR / resource links** — no editorial or .gov/.edu mentions.
- **No local chamber / sponsorship links** — missing city-level authority.

## Recommended Action Sequence

1. **Foundation (Tier A)** — Establish one consistent NAP record and claim GBP, BBB, Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Porch, Nextdoor for each target market.
2. **Industry trust (Tier A/B)** — Join PHCC and any relevant state/local plumbing contractors association; list the business in member directories.
3. **Citations (Tier B)** — Fill Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, BuildZoom, Best Plumbers, Facebook, Manta, Yellow Pages, Superpages, MapQuest.
4. **Local authority (Tier B)** — Join the strongest local chambers of commerce in the top 5 opportunity cities.
5. **Editorial / resource (Tier A/B long term)** — Create genuinely useful resource content (winter plumbing prep, water heater efficiency, burst-pipe emergency checklist) and pitch to local utilities, .gov emergency pages, university housing offices, and home improvement publications.
6. **PR (Tier B)** — Develop local PR hooks (storm response, water conservation partnership, 24/7 service launch) for local news.

---

## Important Constraints

- **No link building, outreach, or purchases should occur** until a real business address and official license numbers are available.
- Many Tier A and B opportunities (BBB, PHCC, local citations, GBP) are **not accessible without a verifiable physical business entity**.
- Do **not** create fake listings, fake addresses, or fake reviews — this would violate Google guidelines and damage trust.

---

## Deliverable Summary

This is the complete authority acquisition strategy. The next step is to secure the real business infrastructure (NAP, licenses, addresses) needed to claim the Tier A foundation, then execute the sequence above.
